CIRCULAR A - ORIGINAL ACADEMIC POLICY

FICTIONAL TEST DATA. This document was created only to demonstrate CONTRADIX and does not describe any real institution.

1. Identification and Attendance

All students must carry their identity cards while on campus.

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% to be eligible for the end-semester examinations.

2. Final Year Project

The project report must be submitted by November 15.

The project must be submitted individually by each student.

No late submissions will be permitted under any circumstances.

3. General Information

The examination hall will open 30 minutes before the start of each examination.

The college library is open from 9 AM to 5 PM on working days.
