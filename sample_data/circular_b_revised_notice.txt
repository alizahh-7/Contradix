CIRCULAR B - REVISED ACADEMIC NOTICE

FICTIONAL TEST DATA. This document was created only to demonstrate CONTRADIX and does not describe any real institution.

1. Identification and Attendance

All students are required to carry their ID cards while on campus.

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 65% to be eligible for the end-semester examinations.

2. Final Year Project

The project report must be submitted by November 20.

The project must be submitted in teams of two students.

Late submissions will be permitted within three days of the deadline.

3. General Information

The examination hall will open 30 minutes before the start of each examination.

The college library is open from 9 AM to 5 PM on working days.
